* In the late 1990s, technology companies were particularly strong advocates of the view that all of their earnings should be plowed back into the business, where they could earn higher returns than any outside shareholder possibly could by reinvesting the same cash if it were paid out to him or her in dividends. Incredibly, investors never questioned the truth of this patronizing Daddy-Knows-Best principleor even realized that a companys cash belongs to the shareholders, not its managers.
