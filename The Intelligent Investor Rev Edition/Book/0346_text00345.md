Note carefully what Graham is saying here. It is not just possible, but probable, that most of the stocks you own will gain at least 50% from their lowest price and lose at least 33% from their highest priceregardless of which stocks you own or whether the market as a whole goes up or down. If you cant live with thator you think your portfolio is somehow magically exempt from itthen you are not yet entitled to call yourself an investor. (Graham refers to a 33% decline as the equivalent one-third because a 50% gain takes a $10 stock to $15. From $15, a 33% loss [or $5 drop] takes it right back to $10, where it started.)
