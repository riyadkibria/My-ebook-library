The bond-discount asset appears to mean that LTV had purchased some bonds below their par value and was treating that discount as an asset, on the grounds that the bonds could eventually be sold at par. Graham scoffs at this, since there is rarely any way to know what a bonds market price will be on a given date in the future. If the bonds could be sold only at values below par, this asset would in fact be a liability.
