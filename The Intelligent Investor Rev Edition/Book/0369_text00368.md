* Todays equivalent of Grahams scarce exceptions tend to be open-end funds that are closed to new investorsmeaning that the managers have stopped taking in any more cash. While that reduces the management fees they can earn, it maximizes the returns their existing shareholders can earn. Because most fund managers would rather look out for No. 1 than be No. 1, closing a fund to new investors is a rare and courageous step.
