Nearly every mutual fund today is taxed as a regulated investment company, or RIC, which is exempt from corporate income tax so long as it pays out essentially all of its income to its shareholders. In the option that Graham omits to avoid clutter, a fund can ask the SEC for special permission to distribute one of its holdings directly to the funds shareholdersas his Graham-Newman Corp. did in 1948, parceling out shares in GEICO to Graham-Newmans own investors. This sort of distribution is extraordinarily rare.
