* As only the latest proof that the more things change, the more they stay the same, consider that Ryan Jacob, a 29-year-old boy wonder, launched the Jacob Internet Fund at year-end 1999, after producing a 216% return at his previous dot-com fund. Investors poured nearly $300 million into Jacobs fund in the first few weeks of 2000. It then proceeded to lose 79.1% in 2000, 56.4% in 2001, and 13% in 2002a cumulative collapse of 92%. That loss may have made Mr. Jacobs investors even older and wiser than it made him.
