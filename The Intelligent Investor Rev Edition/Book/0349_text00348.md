Net asset value, book value, balance-sheet value, and tangible-asset value are all synonyms for net worth, or the total value of a companys physical and financial assets minus all its liabilities. It can be calculated using the balance sheets in a companys annual and quarterly reports; from total shareholders equity, subtract all soft assets such as goodwill, trademarks, and other intangibles. Divide by the fully diluted number of shares outstanding to arrive at book value per share.
