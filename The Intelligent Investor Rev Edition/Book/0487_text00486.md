Rule 703 of the New York Stock Exchange governs stock splits and stock dividends. The NYSE now designates stock dividends of greater than 25% and less than 100% as partial stock splits. Unlike in Grahams day, these stock dividends may now trigger the NYSEs accounting requirement that the amount of the dividend be capitalized from retained earnings.
