* Note very carefully what Graham is saying here. Writing in 1972, he contends that the period since 1949a stretch of more than 22 yearsis too short a period from which to draw reliable conclusions! With his mastery of mathematics, Graham never forgets that objective conclusions require very long samples of large amounts of data. The charlatans who peddle time-tested stock-picking gimmicks almost always base their findings on smaller samples than Graham would ever accept. (Graham often used 50-year periods to analyze past data.)
