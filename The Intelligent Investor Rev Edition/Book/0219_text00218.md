1 Ask yourself which companys stock would be likely to rise more: one that discovered a cure for a rare cancer, or one that discovered a new way to dispose of a common kind of garbage. The cancer cure sounds more exciting to most investors, but a new way to get rid of trash would probably make more money. See Paul Slovic, Melissa Finucane, Ellen Peters, and DonaldG. MacGregor, The Affect Heuristic, in Thomas Gilovich, Dale Griffin, and Daniel Kahneman, eds., Heuristics and Biases: The Psychology of Intuitive Judgment (Cambridge University Press, New York, 2002), pp. 397420, and Donald G. MacGregor, Imagery and Financial Judgment, The Journal of Psychology and Financial Markets, vol. 3, no. 1, 2002, pp. 1522.
