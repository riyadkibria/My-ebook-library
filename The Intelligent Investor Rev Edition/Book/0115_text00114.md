2 Kevin Landis interview on CNN In the Money, November 5, 1999, 11 A. M . eastern standard time. If Landiss own record is any indication, focusing on the things that you know is not all you really need to do to pick stocks successfully. From the end of 1999 through the end of 2002, Landiss fund (full of technology companies that he claimed to know firsthand from his base in Silicon Valley) lost 73.2% of its value, an even worse pounding than the average technology fund suffered over that period.
