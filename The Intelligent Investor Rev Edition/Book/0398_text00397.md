* Graham is referring to the provision of Federal tax law that allows corporations to carry forward their net operating losses. As the tax code now stands, these losses can be carried forward for up to 20 years, reducing the companys tax liability for the entire period (and thus raising its earnings after tax). Therefore, investors should consider whether recent severe losses could actually improve the companys net earnings in the future.
