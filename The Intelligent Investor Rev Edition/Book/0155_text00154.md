1 Sector funds specializing in almost every imaginable industry are availableand date back to the 1920s. After nearly 80 years of history, the evidence is overwhelming: The most lucrative, and thus most popular, sector of any given year often turns out to be among the worst performers of the following year. Just as idle hands are the devils workshop, sector funds are the investors nemesis.
