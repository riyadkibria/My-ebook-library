19 Throughout his writings, Graham insists that corporate managements have a duty not just to make sure their stock is not undervalued, but also to make sure it never gets overvalued. As he put it in Security Analysis (1934 ed., p. 515), the responsibility of managements to act in the interest of their shareholders includes the obligation to preventin so far as they are ablethe establishment of either absurdly high or unduly low prices for their securities. Thus, enhancing shareholder value doesnt just mean making sure that the stock price does not go too low; it also means ensuring that the stock price does not go up to unjustifiable levels. If only the executives of Internet companies had heeded Grahams wisdom back in 1999!
