4 To determine whether a company is an OPM addict, read the Statement of Cash Flows in the financial statements. This page breaks down the companys cash inflows and outflows into operating activities, investing activities, and financing activities. If cash from operating activities is consistently negative, while cash from financing activities is consistently positive, the company has a habit of craving more cash than its own businesses can produceand you should not join the enablers of that habitual abuse. For more on Global Crossing, see the commentary on Chapter 12. For more on WorldCom, see the sidebar in the commentary on Chapter 6.
