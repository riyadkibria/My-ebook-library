Recently, finance professors Owen Lamont of the University of Chicago and Paul Schultz of the University of Notre Dame have shown that corporations choose to offer new shares to the public when the stock market is near a peak. For technical discussion of these issues, see Lamonts Evaluating Value Weighting: Corporate Events and Market Timing and Schultzs Pseudo Market Timing and the Long-Run Performance of IPOs at http:// papers.ssrn.com.
